Learn about this test

A two-sample t-test compares the averages of two groups. It estimates whether their difference could plausibly be due to random variation.

It assumes observations are suitably independent and does not explain why a change happened. Time-series values may be autocorrelated; for seasonal data, match seasons or account for seasonality. A low p-value alone does not establish a cause.
